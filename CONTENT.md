## 10. FAQs

**Heading (H2)**
Frequently Asked Questions

**Q. Who are the Fellowship Programmes for?**
The Fellowship Programmes are intended for PG-qualified doctors seeking advanced, focused clinical training.

**Q. Who are the Post-MBBS Certification Programmes for?**
These programmes are intended for doctors who have completed MBBS and are looking to develop focused clinical skills through structured training.

**Q. What is the fee for the Fellowship Programmes?**
The listed course fee is ₹10,00,000 + 18% GST.

**Q. What is the fee for the Post-MBBS Certification Programmes?**
The listed course fee is ₹3,00,000 + 18% GST.

**Q. Is a stipend provided?**
Yes. The Fellowship Programmes provide a monthly stipend of ₹1,00,000. For Post-MBBS Certification Programmes, the stipend ranges from ₹45,000 to ₹65,000 per month, depending on the hospital location.

**Q. Where are the programmes conducted?**
Programmes are available across multiple hospital locations in India. Programme and location availability varies, so please contact GooCampusWorld for current availability.

**Q. Who awards the certificate?**
Upon successful completion, candidates will receive a Course Completion Certificate jointly awarded by Apollo Hospitals Enterprise Limited and Apollo Hospitals Educational & Research Foundation (AHERF).

**Q. Can GooCampusWorld help me choose the right programme?**
Yes. GooCampusWorld can help you understand the available options based on your qualification, clinical interests and career objectives.

**Q. Are seats guaranteed after submitting an enquiry?**
No. Programme admission and selection are subject to the applicable eligibility criteria, selection process and seat availability.
